📖 How to Play

🎮 Controls

  • Arrow Keys / WASD: Move your character
  • Mouse / Touch: Aim and attack
  • Q / Special Button: Use Ultimate ability
  • ESC: Pause game

🎯 Game Objectives

  • Defeat waves of enemies to advance through stages
  • Collect coins and equipment to become stronger
  • Survive longer and defeat bosses
  • Unlock achievements and climb the leaderboard

⚡ Special Mechanics

  • Level Up: Defeat enemies to gain experience. Every level-up grants a new ability!
  • Equipment: Find and equip weapons, armor, and accessories to boost stats
  • Ultimate Ability: Fill your ultimate meter and unleash devastating attacks
  • Bosses: Every 5th stage features a powerful boss enemy

💡 Tips & Tricks

  • Keep moving to avoid enemy attacks
  • Prioritize health upgrades early on
  • Use your ultimate when surrounded by enemies
  • Save coins to buy better equipment in the shop
